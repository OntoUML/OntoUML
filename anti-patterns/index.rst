OntoUML Anti-Pattern Catalogue
==============================

This list of anti-patterns was created to help modelers avoid models with unintended and often illogical results. The catalogue comes from earlier OntoUML practice. Some names still use the earlier stereotype vocabulary, such as *formal* for what the current profile calls :ref:`comparative`, and *componentOf* for :ref:`componenthood <componentOf>`.

The patterns remain useful as advisory checks. :ref:`Tonto <tooling>` reports them when ``tonto.antiPatterns`` is enabled. They are not well-formedness constraints of the profile.

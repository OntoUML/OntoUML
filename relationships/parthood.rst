.. _parthood:

Parthood
========

A parthood association links a part to a whole. Its instances are irreflexive and asymmetric, and they satisfy weak supplementation: a whole that has a proper part has at least one other part disjoint from the first. A proper part of a whole cannot have that whole as a part.

Examples include an engine as part of a car, a tree as part of a forest, an English-speaking segment as part of a group of visitors, and a wine portion as part of a larger wine portion.

Which stereotype applies depends on the categories of the relata:

- :ref:`componenthood <componentOf>` between functional complexes
- :ref:`membership <memberOf>` of a substantial in a collective
- :ref:`subCollection <subCollectionOf>` between collectives
- :ref:`subQuantity <subQuantityOf>` between quantities
- :ref:`structural`, :ref:`temporal`, and :ref:`participational` between events

:ref:`Constitution <constitution>` is not parthood. It does not satisfy weak supplementation. Shareability, essentiality, and immutability of parts are part of the theory of parthood. The current profile does not yet expose them as stereotype properties. The :ref:`part-whole` page keeps the earlier account of those distinctions.

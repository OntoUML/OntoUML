Definition
----------

«:ref:`componenthood <componentOf>`» is a :ref:`parthood` relation between two functional complexes. The part plays a functional role with respect to the whole. Besides the formal properties of parthood, componenthood is non-transitive: it is transitive in some cases and fails in others.

An engine is a component of a car. A heart is a component of a circulatory system. A keyboard is a component of a computer. A department is a component of an organization.

.. figure:: /specification-figures/componenthood.svg
   :alt: An engine as a component of a car
   :width: 75%
   :align: center

   Associations categorized as «componenthood».

Both ends are classes restricted exactly to ``{functional complex}``.

The examples below use the earlier name «componentOf».

«:ref:`componentOf`» is a parthood relation between two complexes. Examples include:

A. my hand is part of my arm;
B. a car engine is part of a car;
C. an Arithmetic and Logic Unit (ALU) is part of a Central Process Unit (CPU);
D. a heart is part of a circulatory system.

**Transitivity** holds for certain cases but not for others, it depends on context. «:ref:`componentOf`» relation obeys *weak supplementation principle* (at least 2 parts are required, may be of different types).

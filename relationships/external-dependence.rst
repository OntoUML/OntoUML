.. _externalDependence:

External dependence
===================

«externalDependence» is an :ref:`existential dependence <dependency>` whose instances connect an extrinsic mode to an endurant on which it externally depends. That endurant is mereologically disjoint from the bearer of the mode: it shares no parts with the bearer.

Trust depends on the trusted entity. Love depends on the loved entity. A commitment depends on the entity it is directed at. Admiration depends on the admired entity.

.. figure:: /specification-figures/external-dependence.svg
   :alt: Trust externally depending on the trusted entity
   :width: 80%
   :align: center

   An association categorized as «externalDependence».

Allowed ends
------------

- **Dependent end.** A class restricted exactly to ``{extrinsic mode}``: an :ref:`extrinsicMode`, or a sortal that specializes one.
- **Dependee end.** A class whose instances are concrete individuals. ``restrictedTo`` is a non-empty subset of the concrete individual natures. See :ref:`restrictedTo`.

The inherence of the same mode in its bearer is a separate :ref:`characterization`. The pair is the usual truthmaker of a one-sided :ref:`material` relation such as *trusts* or *loves*.

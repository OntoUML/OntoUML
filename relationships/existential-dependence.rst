Existential dependence
======================

An association in this family links a dependent entity to an entity it depends on. An entity *x* depends existentially on *y* when, as a matter of necessity, *y* exists whenever *x* exists.

A marriage depends on the spouses it unites. A headache depends on the person in whom it inheres. A trust depends on the trusted entity.

The profile specializes this idea with:

- :ref:`characterization`, the inherence of a mode or quality in its bearer
- :ref:`mediation`, the dependence of a relator on each endurant it binds
- :ref:`externalDependence`, the dependence of an extrinsic mode on an entity independent of its bearer

Specific dependence points at one particular individual. Generic dependence points at some instance of a type, whichever it is, which is why an engine can be replaced and the car remain the same car. See :ref:`relations-theory`.

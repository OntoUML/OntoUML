.. _constitution:

Constitution
============

«constitution» connects a concrete individual to another concrete individual. They belong to the same broad ontological category, and they follow different ultimate sortals. The constituted individual derives its basic properties from the constituent.

Constitution relations are irreflexive and asymmetric.

A lump of clay constitutes a statue. A group of musicians constitutes a band. The sum of the reciprocal commitments and claims between two spouses constitutes their marriage. The act of raising one's hand constitutes an act of voting.

.. figure:: /specification-figures/constitution.svg
   :alt: A lump of clay constituting a statue
   :width: 80%
   :align: center

   An association categorized as «constitution».

Constitution and parthood answer different questions. :ref:`Parthood <parthood>` satisfies weak supplementation, so a whole with a proper part has another part disjoint from the first. A statue constituted by a single lump of clay has no such further part. The statue is made of that clay. Constitution is the relation that fits this case.

Constitution and identity also come apart, even though the constituent and the constituted individual overlap in space and time. A lump of clay survives being reshaped, and the statue it constitutes does not. A statue survives the loss of a minor piece and is then constituted by a different lump. A band may be constituted by different groups of musicians over time: replacing the drummer yields a different group and the same band.

Allowed ends
------------

Both ends are classes of concrete individuals. ``restrictedTo`` on each end is a non-empty subset of the concrete individual natures.

Constraints
-----------

**Same broad category.** Substantials are constituted by substantials, aspects by aspects, and events by events.

**Different ultimate sortals.** The two ends do not inherit their identity principle from the same ultimate sortal. Constituent and constituted are of different kinds.

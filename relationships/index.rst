Relationship stereotypes
========================

Associations and a few property stereotypes give OntoUML relations their ontological reading. Endurant associations cover dependence, material and comparative relations, parthood, and constitution. Event, situation, and instantiation associations are specified in :ref:`events`, :ref:`situations`, and :ref:`multilevel`. The theory behind the distinctions is in :ref:`relations-theory`.

.. _patterns:

OntoUML Pattern Catalogue
=========================

OntoUML constructs recur in small configurations: phase partitions, relators and the material relations derived from them, and role mixins. The pages below are the community catalogue of those configurations. A pattern library was planned as part of the profile specification and is not yet written there.

:ref:`Tonto <tooling>` can insert checked examples of these shapes, and of parthood, constitution, causation, and multi-level modeling, with **Tonto: Insert Modeling Pattern...** or ``tonto patterns``.

.. _participational:

Participational parthood
========================

«participational» is a :ref:`parthood` relation between events. The part is maximal with respect to its exclusive dependence on a single endurant. No other part of the event depends exclusively on that same endurant.

A player participation is a participational part of a soccer match. A composer participation is a participational part of an act of composition. John's participation is a participational part of a meeting.

.. figure:: /specification-figures/participational.svg
   :alt: A player's participation as part of a soccer match
   :width: 80%
   :align: center

   Associations categorized as «participational».

Both ends are classes restricted exactly to ``{event}``. The endurant on which the part exclusively depends is connected to the whole event by :ref:`participation`.

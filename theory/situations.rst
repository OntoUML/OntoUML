Situations
==========

A **situation** is a particular configuration of a part of reality that can be understood as a whole. It is a snapshot of how certain endurants, their aspects, and the values of their qualities stand at a given time. David's passport being valid, a king standing in checkmate, a flammable gas exceeding a safety threshold, and an assembly reaching quorum are situations. Situations obtain at a time. They do not unfold as events do, and they do not persist through change as endurants do.

Situations connect endurants to events. A situation **triggers** an event when it activates the dispositions whose manifestation is that event: a hazardous gas concentration triggers an explosion, and a checkmate triggers the end of the game. The occurrence of an event **brings about** a new situation: the explosion brings about a situation in which the enclosed space is damaged. Events map the world from the situations that trigger them to the situations they bring about.

When an event brings about a situation that triggers another event, the former event causes the latter. That causal chain is the basis for historical dependence between events.

OntoUML represents these configurations with :ref:`situation`, and the connections with :ref:`triggering`, :ref:`bringingAbout`, and :ref:`presence`.

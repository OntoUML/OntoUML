Definition
----------

A «:ref:`collective`» is a :ref:`first-order ultimate sortal <ultimate-sortal>` whose instances are collectives: integral wholes whose parts play the same role with respect to the whole. Deck of Cards, Brick Pile, and Visitor Group are collectives. A forest is a collective when every tree in the relevant region is one more member, on a par with the others.

The same word can name either a collective or a functional complex. Band is a collective when its parts are uniformly band members. It is a :ref:`kind` when singer, guitarist, drummer, and bassist are distinct functions. Family and Fleet raise the same choice. Ask whether every member is perceived in the same way by the whole. If the answer is yes, the type is a collective. See :ref:`endurants`.

.. figure:: /specification-figures/collective.svg
   :alt: A deck of cards stereotyped as collective
   :width: 70%
   :align: center

   A class categorized as «collective».

A collective may specialize a :ref:`category` or a :ref:`mixin`. It may be specialized by a :ref:`subkind`, :ref:`role`, :ref:`historicalRole`, or :ref:`phase`. Members are connected by :ref:`membership <memberOf>`. A collective that further structures another collective uses :ref:`subCollection <subCollectionOf>`.

``restrictedTo`` is exactly ``{collective}`` and ``order`` is 1.

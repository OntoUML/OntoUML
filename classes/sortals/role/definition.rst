Definition
----------

A «:ref:`role`» is an anti-rigid sortal that specializes a unique :ref:`ultimate sortal <ultimate-sortal>` and classifies entities contingently, in virtue of a relational condition. Every instance of a given role follows the same identity principle, inherited from that ultimate sortal.

A role applies only while the condition holds. A person is an Employee while an employment holds, and ceases to be an employee when it ends, remaining the same person. Visa-Supporting Contract classifies contracts in the scope of visa applications. Protected Species classifies species in the scope of conservation agreements. Student, Spouse, and Customer-as-a-person are roles of the same kind.

.. figure:: /specification-figures/role.svg
   :alt: Employee and other roles specializing a kind
   :width: 80%
   :align: center

   Classes categorized as «role».

A role may specialize a kind, collective, quantity, relator, intrinsic mode, extrinsic mode, quality, situation, type, subkind, role, historical role, phase, category, mixin, role mixin, or historical role mixin. It may be specialized by a role or a historical role.

The relational condition is usually a :ref:`mediation`, a :ref:`material` association, or another dependence or parthood relation that can start and end. A classification that sticks because of a past event, and is kept after the event, is a :ref:`historicalRole`. A contingent classification based on an intrinsic property, such as age, is a :ref:`phase`. A role played by entities of several identity principles is a :ref:`rolemixin`.

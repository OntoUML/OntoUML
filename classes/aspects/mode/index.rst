.. _mode:

Mode
====

Earlier OntoUML used one «mode» stereotype for aspects that inhere in a bearer and are not projected into a quality structure. The current profile separates that category:

- :ref:`intrinsicMode` depends only on its bearer. Courage, fragility, a headache, and a skill are intrinsic modes.
- :ref:`extrinsicMode` inheres in a bearer and also depends on an entity independent of that bearer. Love, a directed commitment, and a claim are extrinsic modes.

Both are rigid ultimate sortals, both are characterized by a :ref:`characterization`, and only the extrinsic mode has an :ref:`externalDependence`. Qualities remain a separate stereotype, :ref:`quality`, because they can be projected into a quality structure.

The examples and constraints below were written for the single stereotype. Read them as applying to both intrinsic and extrinsic modes, and choose the stereotype according to whether the aspect points beyond its bearer.

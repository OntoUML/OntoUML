Definition
----------

A «:ref:`phasemixin`» is an anti-rigid non-sortal that classifies entities contingently, in virtue of an intrinsic condition. It is the non-sortal counterpart of :ref:`phase`. The instances may follow different identity principles.

Phase mixins are defined in partitions, whose members are alternative intrinsic conditions. Living Animal and Deceased Animal partition Animal by life status. Functional Device and Broken Device partition Device by operational status. Available Resource and Unavailable Resource partition Resource by availability.

.. figure:: /specification-figures/phase-mixin.svg
   :alt: Living and deceased animals as a phase-mixin partition
   :width: 80%
   :align: center

   A class categorized as «phaseMixin».

A phase mixin may specialize a category, mixin, or phase mixin. It may be specialized by a phase or a phase mixin. It is an abstract class. The relational counterpart, for a condition that holds because of a relation, is :ref:`rolemixin`.
